Welcome to the Milky Way

Our galaxy contains hundreds of billions of stars. Select the bright centre, a spiral arm, or the Sun’s neighbourhood in the model to explore.

🌀Spiral galaxy

Curving arms carry stars, gas and dust around the galactic centre.

✨Galactic core

A dense central region of stars surrounds a supermassive black hole.

🌍Our neighbourhood

The Sun sits in a smaller feature called the Orion Arm or Orion Spur.

Educational visualisation: the Milky Way is shown from outside for learning. Its exact shape, distances and motion are simplified; we cannot take a photograph of our galaxy from the outside.
